Analytics and Data

Data is the foundation of every successful digital strategy. It takes the guesswork out of communications and allows us to see exactly how audiences are interacting with a brand.

Below is a deep dive into my analytics and performance marketing work for Evergreen Homestay. This case study demonstrates how I use data to build tracking infrastructures, drive visibility, and successfully compete in highly crowded digital markets.

01

Technical Implementation: Google Tag Manager

Reliable analytics start with accurate tracking. Before spending any marketing budget, I designed and implemented a comprehensive tracking environment for evergreenhomestay.online using Google Tag Manager (GTM).

As shown in my diagnostic workflows, I configured the Conversion Linker and Google Analytics 4 (GA4) tags to fire accurately across the site. Beyond basic page views, I built custom event tags to track specific, high-value user actions—such as clicks out to Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) and direct phone calls. This precise technical foundation ensured that every digital touchpoint was measurable and every campaign could be tied directly to a return on investment.

02

Long-Term Campaign Performance

Managing search campaigns requires a constant balancing act between reach and efficiency. Over a sustained period, my Google Ads strategies generated 252,000 impressions and drove 15,200 clicks to the homestay’s website.

By continuously refining audience targeting, keyword selection, and ad copy, I maintained a highly competitive Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 6.03%. More importantly, I kept the campaigns incredibly cost-effective, achieving an Average Cost Per Click (CPC) of just ₹1.40. This proves that high-intent traffic can be acquired efficiently with the right data-led optimisations.

03

Securing Market Share Against Industry Giants

The hospitality sector is notoriously fierce, dominated by massive OTAs with virtually unlimited marketing budgets. However, smart data application allows independent businesses to carve out their own space and compete directly.

My Auction Insights analysis reveals that Evergreen Homestay secured a 17.82% impression share in the search network. We successfully held our ground alongside global giants like Booking.com, Agoda, and Airbnb.

Furthermore, the data shows we actively outranked major regional platforms like MakeMyTrip and Goibibo. By identifying competitive landscape shifts early, I adjusted our bidding strategies to drive a month-over-month impression share increase from 0% to 18%, capturing vital visibility just as new competitors entered the market.

04

Tracking Real Business Outcomes

Ultimately, the goal of analytics is to prove tangible business impact, not just measure website traffic. I mapped the entire digital user journey to track over 1,400 total conversion actions.

This comprehensive setup tracked diverse user intents, from map directions and lead forms to website interactions. Crucially, it successfully attributed 229 direct phone calls specifically to the Google Business Profile and connected ads.

By segmenting these conversion sources, I provided clear, actionable visibility into exactly which digital efforts were putting heads in beds and driving real-world revenue.
